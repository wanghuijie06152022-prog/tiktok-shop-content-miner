# Evidence-to-creative workflow

Run only after completing the case-learning pack.

## 1. Product and content brief

Collect product images, verified features, price/promotion, market, audience, use cases, differentiators, logistics constraints, and prohibited/unverified claims.

For Black Friday choose:

- `Deal shopping` when the brand/product has price recognition or a credible reference price.
- `Gifting` when price recognition is weak but a recipient, relationship, or holiday occasion creates demand.
- A hybrid may use gifting to attract and a verified deal to convert.

Output functional/emotional value, audience segments, motivations, scenes, and missing facts.

## 2. Creative concepts

Generate six differentiated concepts when useful. Include concept name/source template, audience, insight, English hook, 3–8 slide storyline, product integration/proof, purchase motivation, and reason to act now. Recommend the best three using relevance, clarity, proof, feasibility, and differentiation.

## 3. Storyboard

For every slide specify narrative purpose, concise on-image copy, visual composition, continuity, product visibility, proof, and reason to swipe. Remove slides that add no information, emotion, proof, or momentum. The first slide must communicate audience/problem/result quickly; the last must establish a credible next action.

## 4. Production plan

Use real photography for product details, functional demonstrations, product structure, before/after proof, and actions AI may render incorrectly. Use AI for atmosphere, lightweight scenes, exploration, or backgrounds when product accuracy is preserved.

Lock character, location, lighting, product reference, camera style, holiday details, and negative constraints. Generate one independent 3:4 image at a time. Add text in post-production.

## 5. Copy and conversion layer

Use natural market-specific language. Use stock or promotion urgency only when verified. Match CTA to the true offer. Build hashtags as category + need/occasion + one relevant trend. Verify BGM trends at publishing time and respect commercial permissions.

## 6. Final QA

Check product/offer accuracy, first-frame clarity, causal continuity, product reveal timing, proof before CTA, natural language, visual consistency, ratio/safe text placement, and traceability to evidence. Never invent testimonials, prices, scarcity, functions, or outcomes.

## Delivery

As relevant, return the learning summary, brief, concept comparison, storyboard, visual guide/per-slide prompts, overlay copy/caption/CTA/hashtags/BGM direction, and an A/B test plan changing one variable per test.
