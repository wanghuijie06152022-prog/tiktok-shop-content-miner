# Adaptable prompt modules

Replace bracketed fields and include the distilled learning pack. Do not paste confidential or irrelevant raw records.

## Mine the case library

Analyze the GMV-ranked TikTok Shop case library. Retrieve cases matching [market], [category/product], [content type], [GMV threshold/range], [price band], and [occasion]. Prioritize close analogues, then adjacent cases with transferable mechanisms. For each case record observed GMV evidence, hook, first-frame visual, product reveal timing, BODY sequence, proof, CTA, production style, transferable mechanism, and boundary. Separate observation from inference. Synthesize reusable HOOK → BODY → CTA templates. Do not ideate yet.

## Build the brief

Using the verified product facts and case-learning pack, create a concise Content Brief. Separate functional/emotional value, identify three audiences and motivations, recommend scenes and a campaign route, and list missing facts. Do not invent claims or generate images before confirmation.

## Generate concepts

Using verified facts and learned mechanisms, propose six static-carousel concepts. Include name, source template, audience, insight, English hook, 3–8 slide storyline, product integration, proof, motivation, and reason to act. Recommend three based on relevance, proof, feasibility, and differentiation.

## Create the storyboard

Turn [concept] into a six-slide 3:4 carousel. For each slide provide purpose, concise English copy, visual composition, continuity, product visibility, proof, and reason to swipe. Ensure one causal story, not six advertisements. End with a truthful CTA.

## Diagnose weak conversion

Analyze the creative across stop/hold, swipe/engagement, product click, and purchase. For each stage identify visible strengths, friction, evidence, and the metric needed to confirm it. Compare with close high-GMV templates, then rewrite with the fewest changes likely to improve clarity, proof, and motivation.

## Generate one image

Generate only slide [X] as an independent 3:4 image. Follow the confirmed storyboard/visual guide. Preserve product structure, color, material, and logo; keep character, location, lighting, and camera style consistent. Use realistic actions and leave space for copy. Do not render text, prices, stickers, borders, watermarks, or unverified functionality.
